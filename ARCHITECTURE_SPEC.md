# ARCHITECTURE_SPEC.md — Sasmita Atelier // Parfums Jawi Modern
**Benchmark Reference:** https://www.esenzzia.com/ (Madrid Haute Perfumery)  
**Methodology:** ATM (Amati, Tiru, Modifikasi) with Modern Javanese Botanical Terroir  
**Core Directive:** Quiet Luxury, Restrained Elegance, Zero Slop, 1:1 Architectural Parity  

---

## 1. Brand Concept & Modern Javanese Terroir
- **Brand Identity:** **SASMITA ATELIER** (*Sasmita: Isyarat Halus*).
- **Aesthetic Stance:** Understated European Continental perfumery structure meets sacred Indonesian botanical extraction (Kayu Cendana Mataram, Melati Gambir Keraton, Cengkeh Maluku, Jeruk Purut, Teh Hitam Merbabu, Gula Aren Praline).
- **Packaging Language:** Heavy crystal glass cylinder bottle with black round cap, placed on its circular pedestal lid base, beside a cylindrical matte black paper tube packaging with oversized embossed numerals (**`379`**, **`428`**, **`565`**, **`601`**, **`603`**, **`604`**) and the iconic `EXCLUSIVE` badge.
